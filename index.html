About

Hi, I'm Okpe Stephanie

I'm a Creative designer (UI, Brand and Graphic designs) based in Lagos, Nigeria. I help businesses and creatives build visual identities that actually tell their story, from the logo, to the packaging, to the website that holds it all together.

My background in Computer Science means I think in systems as much as I think in colour and type, so what I design is always built to actually work, on-screen and in the real world.
